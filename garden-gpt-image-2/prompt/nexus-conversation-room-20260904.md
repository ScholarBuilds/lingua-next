# 口语会话空间

用途：透明 VRM 人物背后的本地会话背景。人物与交互文字由运行时渲染。

模式：原生 Image2 工具。风格：二次元场景插画。

Create one wide 3:2 anime background illustration for a language conversation app. A quiet contemporary reading room, eye-level straight-on camera, soft hand-painted Japanese animation background art with clean architectural lines. Warm ivory plaster wall in the central 60 percent, airy pale blue window light from the far left, a small leafy plant and a low bookcase on the far right, light oak floor only along the bottom edge. Restrained cream, desaturated sky blue, sage, and natural wood palette. A little depth, diffuse daylight, no dramatic lighting. Keep the central area uncluttered and softly lit for a separately composited standing anime character; no furniture in the central foreground. Peripheral detail only, calm enough for long conversations. No people, no faces, no silhouettes, no text, no logos, no interface, no icons, no vignette, no photorealism. Full bleed seamless scene, not a framed picture.
