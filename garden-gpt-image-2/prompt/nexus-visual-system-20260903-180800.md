# NEXUS visual system

## Domain illustration atlas

Create a production asset sheet for NEXUS, a personal desktop learning and creative workbench. Use a precise 4 by 4 grid containing exactly sixteen square illustrations, with equal gutters and no labels, words, numerals, logos, borders, or watermarks. Each cell must be visually self-contained and safe to crop at its exact grid boundary.

Cell order, left to right and top to bottom:

1. Today: a sunlit desk, calendar page, and one blue pencil.
2. Reading: an open clothbound book with a slim bookmark.
3. Video: a small cinema frame with a play triangle and subtitle strip.
4. Vocabulary: three index cards with one highlighted word line.
5. Grammar: sentence fragments connected by precise editorial brackets.
6. Conversation: two speech forms and a tabletop microphone.
7. Studio: a cutting mat with image, text, and film elements arranged for making.
8. Mail: a folded letter and sealed envelope.
9. Accounts: a brass key beside a compact safe drawer.
10. Extensions: modular plugs fitting into a rail.
11. Tasks: a checklist beside a small mechanical timer.
12. Settings: a calibrated dial and two adjustment rails.
13. Image creation: a framed landscape being painted by a light beam.
14. Workflow: connected paper cards forming a clear process path.
15. Canvas: layered paper, ruler, crop marks, and movable pins.
16. Asset library: an archival drawer containing image, film, audio, and document sleeves.

Visual language: editorial product illustration, Japanese stationery precision mixed with restrained Swiss information design; warm ivory paper, deep navy ink, cobalt blue as the only strong accent, tiny amber details only where useful. Subtle paper grain, tactile cut-paper depth, crisp edges, soft daylight from upper left, restrained shadows. Consistent camera angle, material scale, stroke weight, and illumination across all cells. Calm, intelligent, useful, and mature rather than cute or futuristic. No purple gradients, neon, glassmorphism, glossy 3D app icons, emoji, characters, floating orbs, decorative sparkles, fake UI text, or photorealistic hands. Square 2048 by 2048, transparent outer background if supported.

Create a seamless, extremely subtle background texture for a desktop productivity application: warm ivory archival paper with barely visible fibers, faint cool-blue drafting grid lines at very low opacity, and occasional tiny registration marks near the far edges. Flat, even illumination, no objects, no text, no logo, no vignette, no gradient blobs, no noise clumps, no shadows. It must remain quiet behind dense user-interface content and tile seamlessly. Square 2048 by 2048.

Create a production UI glyph sheet for NEXUS. Use a precise 6 by 6 grid containing exactly thirty-six centered glyphs, equal cell sizes, generous transparent padding, and no dividers, labels, words, numerals, logos, shadows, tiles, buttons, backgrounds, or watermarks. Every glyph must be isolated and safe to crop at its exact grid boundary.

Cell order, left to right and top to bottom: back arrow, forward arrow, up arrow, down arrow, close, check; plus, minus, search, more-horizontal, refresh, play; pause, download, upload, copy, edit-pencil, delete-trash; save, folder, document, image, video-film, audio-wave; link, settings-sliders, information, warning-triangle, lock, key; grid, list, crop, magic-wand, cloud, workflow-nodes.

Style: custom editorial instrument glyphs rather than generic Lucide icons. Deep navy ink with a restrained cobalt detail, consistent 2.25-pixel visual stroke at final 24-pixel size, subtly squared terminals, slightly softened corners, strong recognizable silhouettes, optical centering, same apparent weight across all symbols. Flat vector-like rendering with transparent background. No 3D, gradients, glow, emoji, skeuomorphic materials, filled circles behind icons, decorative sparkles, tiny incidental details, or text. Square 2048 by 2048.
