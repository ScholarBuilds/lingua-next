## Video library

The bundled Welcome to NEXUS lesson uses original text, NEXUS artwork, and locally
synthesized narration. English cue timings are approximate; Chinese translations
are included. Downloaded third-party videos and their subtitles are not bundled.
`starter-content/video-sources.json` lists public source links for reference;
users must obtain any additional materials according to their own rights.

marks those locations; the author's private vault is not part of this repository.
